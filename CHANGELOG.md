# @boxofrules/assets changelog

## 1.1.1 (19 Sep 2026)

Box Of Synths full, card and logotype relaid (James): the keys sit under BOX OF SYNTHS, and the wordmark sits 28 units from the ring's outer dot instead of 68. Letters and keys unchanged, only moved; the full logo's canvas is 884 x 290. White variants follow.

## 1.1.0 (19 Sep 2026)

The font standard, defined here for every site: Syne for headings, Barlow for everything else (James, 19 Sep 2026). Syne ships as the variable woff2 with full coverage, Barlow at 400, 500, 600, 700, both with their OFL texts. Anton and Oswald leave the kit; the tokens gain `--font-heading` with `--font-display` as a legacy alias. The Waveform Analyser and Synth.Directory wordmarks are outlined too, so no mark in the kit references a font.

The sign, fixed. Every product icon and full logo had carried a redrawn approximation of the Box Of Rules sign from the Synths logo pass (heavier B bar, different R leg); all now embed the exact master path at the same size and position. Box Of Synths gains its full (sign + keys + BOX OF SYNTHS), card (1000x600, the padded 5:3 version for og and social) and logotype files in green and white. One vocabulary for every product: full, icon, logotype, card. Renames: plugins lockup -> full, mark -> icon; waveform-analyser likewise; synth-directory mark -> icon; box-of-synths mark-* -> icon-*. 23 marks.

## 1.0.1 (19 Sep 2026)

Renamed from @boxofrules/brand: the repo is boxofrules/assets and the remit widens to every approved asset (plugin imagery next). No file moved.

## 1.0.0 (19 Sep 2026)

First release: the vault masters as a package. 17 marks across Box Of Rules, plugins, Box Of Synths, Synth.Directory and Waveform Analyser; Anton, Oswald and Barlow; core and per-product tokens; the manifest and the rules.
