# @boxofrules/assets changelog

## Unreleased (1.2.0)

- `products/plugins/<slug>/<version>/`: the approved imagery for every shipped plugin at its shipped version (Box Of Bass 1.2.2, Picking Fingers 1.1.0, Focus 1.0.1, BR108 1.0.0): the interface shot, galleries, bundle icon, Anagram block, og card, home band and release post, taken from what plugins.boxofrules.com serves today. `latest/` beside the versions is the copy to link. Listed in the manifest's `products` section; `product(slug, file, version)` in `index.js` resolves them and `--check` covers them.
- Favicons fill the tile: the sign takes 92% of the small favicons and 84% of the app icons (was 62%, a dot in a browser tab). Plugins and Waveform Analyser, whose icons are wide, get the sign alone in the product colour for the 16/32/48 sizes.
- The repo is public and the package is unscoped for npm (`@boxofrules/assets`, access public). `LICENSE.md`: fonts OFL, marks and product imagery all rights reserved with a permission to use them unmodified to refer to Box Of Rules, tokens and code MIT.
- README rewritten as a guide: what is in the package, the rules, how each kind of consumer takes it.
- The CDN: R2 bucket `bor-assets` behind `cdn.boxofrules.com`, CORS for the Box Of Rules domains. `tools/publish-cdn.sh` and the `publish-cdn` workflow push every release to `/v<tag>/` (immutable) and `/latest/`. The current working set is on `/latest/` from 19 Sep.
- `web/<product>/`: favicons (svg, ico, 16, 32), apple-touch-icon, PWA icons 192/512 and a maskable 512, `site.webmanifest` and a `head.html` snippet for all six products, generated by `tools/web-icons.py` from each product's icon on a white circle. Sites pull these from the package instead of drawing their own.
- Synth.Directory mark redrawn with James from his Frame 4: a vertical fader with notches and a clay dot at zero as the dot between S and D (icon) and between Synth and Directory (full); Barlow 700 and 400 throughout, the site's own light and dark wordmark colours; full, card, icon, logotype in both themes with PNGs. The Barlow files in the kit are now full builds from the Google Fonts source: the copy taken from synth.directory's server (Barlow 700) was a subset missing every letter.
- New product `accounts` ("My Box Of", the account hub): the Box Of Rules sign as icon, MY BOX OF… (Syne's ellipsis glyph) in Syne 700, on the plugins layout rule (cap height = the hex's right edge). Full, card, icon, logotype in blue and white. Unbounded joins `fonts/` as the plugins-only heading face with its OFL; the manifest gains a `typography` section per product.
- Plugins full logo relaid by James in Figma: the icon at the letters' cap height, the wave through the icon's notches and on under PLUGINS, ending just past the S; the letters' ink outline removed. The plugins icon is James's Frame 2 (wave in, sign, wave out). Plugins now has all four forms, full, card, icon, logotype, SVG and PNG.
- Box Of Rules and Box Of Synths brought to the same four forms: full, card, icon, logotype. Box Of Rules gains its cards (blue, white, black).
- Every Box Of Rules and Box Of Synths SVG is cropped to its content: background rects removed, viewBox = bounding box, no margin. Cards are rebuilt on one rule for both: the full logo fitted to an 880 x 290 box centred in 1000 x 600.
- The old Box Of Rules PNGs (padded, with backgrounds) are gone; every Box Of Rules and Box Of Synths mark now ships a transparent, margin-free PNG at 1024 px on the long edge, listed in the manifest.
- Box Of Synths full, card and logotype relaid with James: keys under BOX OF SYNTHS, edge to edge with the letters; cap line on the hex's top-right corner; wordmark 28 units from the ring.

## 1.1.0 (19 Sep 2026)

The font standard, defined here for every site: Syne for headings, Barlow for everything else (James, 19 Sep 2026). Syne ships as the variable woff2 with full coverage, Barlow at 400, 500, 600, 700, both with their OFL texts. Anton and Oswald leave the kit; the tokens gain `--font-heading` with `--font-display` as a legacy alias. The Waveform Analyser and Synth.Directory wordmarks are outlined too, so no mark in the kit references a font.

The sign, fixed. Every product icon and full logo had carried a redrawn approximation of the Box Of Rules sign from the Synths logo pass (heavier B bar, different R leg); all now embed the exact master path at the same size and position. Box Of Synths gains its full (sign + keys + BOX OF SYNTHS), card (1000x600, the padded 5:3 version for og and social) and logotype files in green and white. One vocabulary for every product: full, icon, logotype, card. Renames: plugins lockup -> full, mark -> icon; waveform-analyser likewise; synth-directory mark -> icon; box-of-synths mark-* -> icon-*. 23 marks.

## 1.0.1 (19 Sep 2026)

Renamed from @boxofrules/brand: the repo is boxofrules/assets and the remit widens to every approved asset (plugin imagery next). No file moved.

## 1.0.0 (19 Sep 2026)

First release: the vault masters as a package. 17 marks across Box Of Rules, plugins, Box Of Synths, Synth.Directory and Waveform Analyser; Anton, Oswald and Barlow; core and per-product tokens; the manifest and the rules.
